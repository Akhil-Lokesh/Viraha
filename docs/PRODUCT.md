# Viraha — Product

**Viraha** (Sanskrit: विरह) is the ache of separation from what you love. The app exists for
the places you've loved and left: to keep them close, and to find new ones through people
whose taste you trust.

## The problem

- **Memories fragment.** Photos scatter across devices and lose the story, the feeling and
  the exact place.
- **Discovery is noisy.** Review sites flatten places into star ratings; social feeds are
  beautiful but context-free.

## Principles

1. **Geography is the framework.** Every memory is pinned to a place. The map is a primary
   way to browse, not an afterthought.
2. **People over algorithms.** You discover through travelers you follow. No star ratings,
   no scores.
3. **Story, place and photo carry equal weight.** Not photo-only like Instagram, not
   text-only like a blog.
4. **Context-aware.** The app behaves differently at home and on the road.
5. **Private by choice.** Every post, album and journal has its own privacy, and precise
   locations can be hidden.

## What you can do

**Capture**
- **Posts** — the atomic memory: up to 10 photos, a caption, a tagged place (search or exact
  coordinates), tags, date taken, travel mode, privacy, comments on/off, exact or approximate location.
- **Albums** — curated collections of posts.
- **Journals** — long-form writing with a rich-text editor; drafts until published.
- **Scrapbooks** — free-form collections of notes and links.
- **Time capsules** ("Seal a memory") — a memory that opens on a future date.

**Relive**
- **Home** — your latest memory as the hero, your stats, and a customizable widget board
  (on this day, places that miss you, seasonal reflections, streaks, mini-map, and more).
- **Map** — every memory as a pin; filter posts/journals and everyone/mine, scrub through
  time, find what's nearby.
- **Atlas** — your travel DNA: countries, cities and continents visited, your travel style.
- **Journeys** — trips detected automatically from your posts, shown as timelines.
- **Want to go** — a list of places you dream about.

**Discover**
- **Explore** — a Following feed and a For You feed, trending destinations, search for
  places and travelers, and a map of what the community has posted.
- **Profiles** — a traveler's posts, albums, journals and map.
- **Travel mode** — when you're far from home, Viraha offers to switch to traveling mode so
  discovery follows where you are.

**Connect**
- Follow travelers (private accounts approve requests), like, comment and reply, save posts.
- **Activity** — realtime notifications for follows, likes, comments and saves.
- Mute or block anyone; report content; manage active sessions; export or delete your data.

## Who it's for

Thoughtful travelers who want to document trips meaningfully and are tired of performative
feeds; frequent travelers and nomads who want trusted, place-anchored recommendations.

## Voice

Thoughtful, not rushed. Authentic, not performative. Minimal, not cluttered. Warm, not cold.
